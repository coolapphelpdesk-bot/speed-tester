Privacy Policy

Application: Speed Tester (package coolapp.speedtest) · Effective date: 2026-05-11

This Privacy Policy describes how the Speed Tester Android application ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data about you. The only data exchanged with us-controlled systems is the test traffic required to measure your connection. The App does, however, display advertising provided by Google AdMob, and Google's SDK processes a limited set of device data described below.

1. Data we collect directly

We (the developer of Speed Tester) do not collect any personal data. Specifically, the App does not collect, log, or transmit to us:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any identifier you provide.
  • Your IMEI, MAC address, or other persistent hardware identifiers.
  • Your precise or coarse location.
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, or camera data.
  • Crash reports, analytics events, or usage telemetry.

The App contains no first-party analytics SDKs and no tracking libraries beyond the Google AdMob advertising SDK described in section 4.

2. Data we store on your device

The result of your most recent speed test (ping, download speed, upload speed, timestamp, and the public name of the test server) is held in the App's memory while the App is open, so it can be shown on the home screen. This data is never transmitted off the device and is discarded when the system reclaims the process.

The App ships with a bundled list of public Speedtest.net server endpoints (speedtest-servers.csv) used to pick a low-latency test target. This list is read locally and is never updated over the network.

3. Network traffic the App generates

To measure your connection, the App opens HTTPS connections to a small number of well-known public speed-test endpoints and exchanges bytes with them:

EndpointPurposeWhat it sees
speed.cloudflare.comLatency, upload, and fallback download measurementYour public IP address, standard HTTP headers (User-Agent, the bytes uploaded/downloaded during the test)
speed.hetzner.dePrimary download measurementYour public IP address, standard HTTP headers
proof.ovh.netDownload measurement (fallback only)Your public IP address, standard HTTP headers

These third parties operate the servers you transfer test bytes with. Like any HTTP request, your public IP address is visible to them. The App sends only the data required for the speed measurement — no identifiers, no account information, no device metadata beyond the User-Agent string. Each of these providers has its own privacy policy:

  • Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • Hetzner: https://www.hetzner.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • OVH: https://www.ovh.com/world/protection-personal-data/

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. Two ad formats are used:

  • Interstitial ads shown occasionally after a completed speed test (at most once every two tests, and never within sixty seconds of the previous ad).
  • Rewarded video ads shown only when you tap the optional "Watch a short video → ad-free for 24 h" button. Watching this video disables interstitial ads for twenty-four hours.

To serve these ads, Google AdMob processes data on its own, as an independent data controller. The data Google may receive includes:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier you control in your device settings).
  • Your IP address.
  • General device information (model, OS version, language, time zone).
  • Information about ad interactions (impressions, clicks, completion of rewarded videos).
  • An approximate location derived from your IP address.

Google uses this data to serve, measure, and target advertising, as described in Google's own privacy policies. We do not receive any of this information; AdMob shares only aggregated revenue reports with us.

You can:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising on the same screen ("Delete advertising ID" on Android 12+, or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older versions).
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App shows a consent prompt powered by Google's User Messaging Platform on first launch where you can decline personalised ads. Non-personalised ads will still be shown.

References:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites/apps that use their services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob & AdSense data disclosures: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

6. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. AdMob is configured to serve only non-personalised ads to users who appear to be in regions where Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA, GDPR-K) applies via the consent prompt described in section 4.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or rent any data. We do not directly receive any user data to share. The processing of advertising data by Google AdMob is governed by Google's own policies linked in section 4.

8. Security

All speed-test traffic uses HTTPS (TLS). The App does not store credentials or persistent identifiers locally.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data, this policy will be updated and the "Effective date" at the top of the page will change. Continued use of the App after such a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
